## 3. Business Understanding

This project analyzes inventory, demand, supplier, and warehouse
performance across a simulated supply chain network.

The objective is to identify inventory efficiency issues,
replenishment risks, supplier lead-time variability, and
forecasting performance.

### Business Rules & Assumptions

#### Supplier Lead-Time Segmentation

Supplier lead times are segmented into three analytical categories
based on the observed distribution of the dataset:

| Category | Lead Time |
|---|---:|
| Fast | 2–4 days |
| Normal | 5–10 days |
| Slow | 11–14 days |

The thresholds are derived from the observed lower and upper
quartile boundaries of the dataset. These categories are used for
analytical segmentation and do not represent contractual supplier
SLAs or operational policies.
