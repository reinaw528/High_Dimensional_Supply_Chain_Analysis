# Supply Chain Inventory Analytics Project

## Business Background

A company operates multiple warehouses and suppliers across different regions.

Management faces challenges in:

- Excess inventory
- Inventory turnover inefficiency
- Forecast inaccuracies
- Supplier lead time variability

The goal of this project is to build an executive dashboard that provides visibility into inventory health, demand forecasting performance, and supplier effectiveness.

## Key Business Questions

1. How efficiently is inventory being utilized?

2. Which warehouses hold excessive inventory?

3. How accurate are demand forecasts?

4. Which suppliers present operational risks?

5. What actions should management take to improve supply chain performance?
