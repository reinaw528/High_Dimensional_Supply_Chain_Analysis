# Dashboard Design

## Executive Overview

Purpose:
Provide a high-level view of inventory health and operational performance.

KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Gross Margin
- Inventory Turnover
- Avg Forecast Accuracy
- Avg Lead Time


Visuals:
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Forecast Accuracy Trend
- Top SKU by Profit
- Total Profit by Warehouse
- Avg Inventory Trend

---

## Inventory Analysis

Purpose:
Identify inventory imbalance and replenishment opportunities.

KPIs:
- Inventory Value
- Days of Inventory (DOI)
- Avg Daily Inventory
- Inventory Turnover
- At-Risk SKU Count

Visuals:
- Inventory Trend by Quarter
- Inventory Turnover by Warehouse
- Top At-Risk SKU
- DOI by Warehouse
- Regional Gap Analysis

---
## Demand & Forest Performance

Purpose:
Evaluate demand and forecast performance.

KPIs:
- Total Demand
- Forecast Accuracy
- BIas
- WAPE

Visuals:
- Actual & Forecast Demand
- Forecast Accuracy Trend
- BIas Warehouse
- WAPE by month
- Forecast Error by Month

## Supplier Performance

Purpose:
Evaluate supplier reliability and performance.

KPIs:
- Active Suppliers
- Avg Lead Time
- Highest Risk Supplier Count
- Lowest Supplier Accuracy
- Suppliers Meeting Target

Visuals:
- Longest Lead Time Suppliers
- Supplier Risk Matrix
- Forecast Accuracy Ranking
- Supplier Risk & Performance
